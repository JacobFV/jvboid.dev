## What the behavior means

The motor probe is a legitimate metacognitive experiment. It deliberately changes the body's input so the controller can estimate the mapping between commanded and realized motion. The resulting update reduces uncertainty about the robot's own actuator dynamics rather than about an external object.

But a meaningful self-test is not automatically valuable. G+ and even SA gate-off can compensate through ordinary closed-loop feedback. Across 20 paired evaluations, SA's mean utility difference is $-0.085$ with interval $[-0.207, 0.039]$.

The result should be read as a boundary condition: explicit self-modeling is unnecessary when the base controller already observes enough error, adapts quickly, and faces no catastrophic consequence for transient imbalance.

## Next experiment

The diagnostic should be repeated with fall dynamics, payload shifts, asymmetric friction, irreversible damage, and a narrow stability envelope. Those conditions increase the value of predicting future failure *before* ordinary feedback experiences it.
