The asterisk is deliberate. Wigner wrote *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of
Mathematics in the Natural Sciences*, and the title turned into a template —
unreasonable effectiveness of data, of recurrent neural networks, of
mathematics in biology, of scale. The template survived because the underlying
puzzle keeps recurring in new substrates.

Wigner's version: the mathematical concepts physicists reach for were chosen for
elegance, not for fit, and they fit anyway — to absurd precision, in domains
they were never designed for. He declines to explain it. He calls it a
"wonderful gift which we neither understand nor deserve."

What I take from the whole `*` family is a working heuristic: when a
representation keeps paying off far outside the regime it was fit on, that
over-generalization is the finding. It means the representation caught
structure, not surface.
