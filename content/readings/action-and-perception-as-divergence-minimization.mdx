Perception fits beliefs to the world; action fits the world to beliefs. This
paper writes both as the same move — shrinking one joint KL divergence — and
then shows how much of the zoo of agent objectives is that move with different
terms held fixed. It is the free energy principle made concrete enough to
build with.
