Every other lament in the psalter turns somewhere near the end — *but I will
trust*, *but you have heard*. Psalm 88 does not turn. It ends on the word
darkness and leaves you there.

I keep coming back to it because it is the honest counterweight to every
optimistic thing I believe about building. Not everything resolves inside the
frame you can see. A tradition that keeps this psalm in the book is a tradition
willing to hold an unresolved case without editing it into a lesson.
