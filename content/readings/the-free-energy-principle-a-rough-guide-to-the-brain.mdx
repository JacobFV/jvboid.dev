The best on-ramp Friston has written. The later formulations are more complete
and much harder to enter; this one gets to the load-bearing idea in a few pages
with a glossary attached.

The idea: a system that persists must resist the dispersion of its own sensory
states, which means minimizing surprise, which it cannot compute — so it
minimizes a variational bound on surprise instead. Free energy. Then perception
is minimizing that bound by changing the model, action is minimizing it by
changing the sensory input, and attention is the precision-weighting that
decides which prediction errors get to matter.

Three separate faculties collapsing into one quantity is the kind of
consolidation I look for. It sits directly upstream of [A Beautiful
Loop](/readings/a-beautiful-loop), which asks what has to be added to this
machinery before it becomes an account of consciousness rather than of
inference.
