The graduation-gift reading of this book is the wrong one. It is not a
congratulations card — most of its pages are about failure modes.

The Waiting Place is the best thing in it: a whole population stuck waiting for
a phone to ring or a better break or a Yes-or-No, and the book's verdict is that
this is the one place you must not stay. Then the slump, the Lurch, the
unslumping that is not easily done, and the honest arithmetic at the end —
98¾ percent guaranteed, which is not a hundred.

It stays on the list because it is the shortest correct statement of the thing:
the failure mode is not losing, it is waiting.
