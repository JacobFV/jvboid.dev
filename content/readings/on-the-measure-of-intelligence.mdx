This belongs near the center of the reading list because it asks the right
measurement question: not "how much benchmark skill did a system buy with data,
compute, and priors?" but "how efficiently can it acquire new skill under
controlled assumptions?"

The useful pressure point is the separation between performance, prior
knowledge, experience, scope, and generalization difficulty.
