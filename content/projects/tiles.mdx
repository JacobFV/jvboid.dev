- Free and open source matching game — clear the board by pairing tiles that share a **shape or a color**
- Single self-contained `index.html`: plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no build step, no dependencies
- Tunable difficulty — grid size, shape count, color palette, and an optional time limit
- Combo scoring with a locally-stored high score, plus a one-tap share card
