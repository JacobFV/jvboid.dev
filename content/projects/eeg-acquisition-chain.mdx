## Problem

EEG hardware decisions are difficult to reason about when cortical sources, volume conduction, electrode contact, front-end electronics, sampling, and artifact compensation are treated as separate diagrams.

## Solution

A compact interactive research tool that exposes the acquisition chain as one continuous simulation, from cortical current dipoles to the converter output.

## How

The client-side React model couples tissue and electrode parameters to complex impedance, noise, filtering, gain, multiplexing, quantization, sensor telemetry, and a deliberately simple nuisance estimator. Every measurement point includes a waveform and scrolling spectrogram.

## Tests

The repository runs a production build plus server-render, physics, and LaTeX validation tests in its GitHub Pages workflow.

## Results

The tool makes signal loss and unrecoverable corruption visible while letting force, displacement, impedance, optical telemetry, and neighboring channels be ablated against the same simulated sequence.

## Lessons

Telemetry can remove observable nuisance; it cannot reconstruct information already destroyed by volume conduction, clipping, aliasing, or the electronics noise floor.
