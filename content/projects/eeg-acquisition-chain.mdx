![EEG spectrogram with a 16 by 16 skull projection, noise-density plot, and signal-chain equations](/assets/img/projects/eeg-acquisition-chain-spectrograph.jpg)

- **Chain.** Cortex → volume conduction → electrode–skin impedance → AFE/PGA → AMUX/ADC, with linked waveforms, spectrograms, complex transfer functions, and noise budgets.
- **Compensation.** Force, displacement, impedance, optical state, and neighboring channels drive a deliberately simple estimator, scored against simulator ground truth through sensor ablations.
- **Boundary.** Observable nuisance can be subtracted; volume-conduction loss, clipping, aliasing, and electronics noise cannot be reconstructed.
