## Project

_The Right Night Light_ is an early Blender animation built around a simple visual premise: a monkey needs the right night light before he can cross the ground. The project sits in the same period as other school-era animations, where the main work was learning how to stage a scene, move characters, and turn a small idea into a finished video.
