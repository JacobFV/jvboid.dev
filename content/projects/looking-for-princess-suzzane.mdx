## Project

_Looking for Princess Suzzane_ is a Spanish-class Blender animation from December 2013. The video uses a narrative frame to teach the conjugation pattern for Spanish -AR verbs, turning a grammar lesson into a short animated story.

It is one of the earliest preserved animation projects on the site: rough, classroom-oriented, and useful as a record of combining language learning with 3D animation before later projects became more technical.
