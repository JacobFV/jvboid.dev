There is something painful about the asymmetry between student memory and teacher memory. A teacher can permanently alter the trajectory of a child through ordinary competent attention and then teach hundreds more children. For me, individual teachers become structural nodes in the autobiography. For them, I was one kid in a class that happened sixteen years ago. That is not unfair. It is just how the graph works. But it means the student can carry an encounter much longer than the adult who generated it.

Mrs. Restivo sits right near a hinge. Before her, school is mostly this engine that keeps rewarding curiosity and effort. During those last years, it becomes explicitly competitive and terminal: class rank, graduation, scholarship decisions, the question of what all this accumulated potential is FOR. She is attached to the last period where the adults around me could look at the metrics and assume the next step was obvious.
